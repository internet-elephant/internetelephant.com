01 — Index

Friendly, experienced IT support.

Internet Elephant provides hands-on help for Linux, Mac, Windows, the web, and anything in the office. From fixing a suddenly broken machine to designing your infrastructure from the ground up.

About

Twenty-five years of practice across home users to small businesses, from hospitals to universities, and Fortune 50 enterprises. The work spans whatever the day asks. A stuck printer, a quiet server room, a fresh deploy, a tangled network closet, or a long conversation about what the team actually needs.

We meet you where you are, whether that's "please just make it work" or "walk me through every layer."

What we do

  • Workstation & server triage: Linux, macOS, Windows
  • Office networks and Wi-Fi, file storage and backups
  • Web hosting, DNS, security certificates, small deploys
  • Quiet long-term sysadmin for teams that need IT on call
